# Puerto Vallarta Holiday Quest
![A colorful illustrated banner of three travelers on a quest through Puerto Vallarta, showing a dotted route connecting a beachside seahorse statue, hillside town, and a swinging bridge to Río Cuale.](cover.png)

A cooperative-competitive mystery scavenger hunt game for families and friends to play around a holiday table.

## Play it
You can play the game live at: [https://your-username.github.io/your-repo-name](https://your-username.github.io/your-repo-name) *(replace this with your actual GitHub Pages link!)*

To play, you just need a single phone, tablet, or computer. The game is designed for exactly 3 players sharing one screen.

## How to play
1. Gather 3 players around a single mobile phone or tablet, then press "Start Quest."
2. The game will show who’s up. Pass the phone to that player for their turn.
3. Look at the two visual "Postcard Stamps" (Zone and Detail) at the center of the card.
4. Review the 4 local Puerto Vallarta landmarks and tap the one that matches both visual clues. 
5. If you are incorrect, you can keep guessing, but take note of the visual clues! If you are correct, your marker advances 1 step, and it is the next player's turn.
6. The first player to complete 4 stops and cross the final bridge to Río Cuale wins the game!
