Puerto Vallarta Holiday Quest

Combine two visual postcard clues each turn to race across Puerto Vallarta and reach Río Cuale first.

  • Pass the phone each turn: Player 1, Player 2, Player 3.
  • Match both clue stamps to tap the real Puerto Vallarta spot.
  • First player to solve 4 stops and cross the bridge to Río Cuale wins!
